# *In Vivo* CAR-T Therapies: One-Page Executive Summary

**Last updated:** April 28, 2026

**Scope note:** This summary covers publicly visible / announced *in vivo* CAR-T developers, transactions, and financings. It is not a complete inventory of stealth, academic, or undisclosed programs.

## Bottom line

*In vivo* CAR-T is strategically important because it targets the biggest operating-model weaknesses of current cell therapy: individualized manufacturing, cost, long timelines, limited scalability, and access constraints. It is not yet a proven replacement for *ex vivo* CAR-T, but it is emerging as a high-option platform bet because it could turn CAR-T into a more manufacturing-light, drug-like product model if delivery works.

## Why the field matters now

The clearest recent validation event was Eli Lilly's 2026 agreement to acquire Kelonia Therapeutics for **up to $7.0 billion in cash**, including **$3.25 billion upfront**.[Lilly/Kelonia](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-acquire-kelonia-therapeutics-advance-vivo-car-t-cell) The deal is directly tied to *in vivo* CAR-T: Kelonia's lead program, **KLN-1010**, is a **Phase 1** anti-BCMA candidate in relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma.[Lilly/Kelonia](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-acquire-kelonia-therapeutics-advance-vivo-car-t-cell) [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07075185](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07075185)

That transaction followed deals involving Orna, Orbital, Capstan, Interius, and EsoBiotec, reinforcing the view that large pharma wants delivery-platform exposure before full clinical de-risking.[Lilly/Orna](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-acquire-orna-therapeutics-advance-cell-therapies) [BMS/Orbital](https://news.bms.com/news/corporate-financial/2025/Bristol-Myers-Squibb-Strengthens-and-Diversifies-Cell-Therapy-Portfolio-with-Acquisition-of-Orbital-Therapeutics/default.aspx) [AbbVie/Capstan](https://news.abbvie.com/2025-06-30-AbbVie-to-Acquire-Capstan-Therapeutics%2C-Further-Strengthening-Commitment-to-Transforming-Patient-Care-in-Immunology) [Kite/Interius](https://investors.gilead.com/news/news-details/2025/Kite-to-Acquire-Interius-BioTherapeutics-to-Advance-In-Vivo-Platform/default.aspx) [EsoBiotec/AstraZeneca](https://www.esobiotec.com/esobiotec-to-be-acquired-by-astrazeneca-to-advance-cell-therapy-ambition/)

## Key takeaways

- **Why the space matters now:** *in vivo* CAR-T is being valued as a route to reduce manufacturing complexity, shorten timelines, and broaden access, not just as another CAR format.[FDA CAR-T class overview](https://www.fda.gov/safety/medical-product-safety-information/bcma-directed-or-cd19-directed-autologous-chimeric-antigen-receptor-car-t-cell-immunotherapies-fda)
- **What it is trying to change:** the model replaces apheresis, external engineering, and reinfusion with targeted delivery of CAR instructions inside the patient.[Li et al., PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41928252/) [Bot et al., Nature Reviews Drug Discovery](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41573-025-01291-5)
- **What still has to work:** targeted *in vivo* gene delivery remains the chokepoint because the system must reach the right immune cells with enough safety, control, and durability to matter clinically.
- **Where the ecosystem is concentrating:** targeted LNP / RNA and retargeted lentiviral vectors are the most visible delivery archetypes, while autoimmune disease and oncology remain the main use-case clusters.[Capstan Phase 1 start](https://www.capstantx.com/press-releases/capstan-therapeutics-announces-initiation-of-phase-1-trial-of-lead-in-vivo-car-t-therapy-cptx2309-for-treating-autoimmune-disease/) [Kelonia ASH 2025](https://keloniatx.com/kelonia-therapeutics-presents-first-in-human-data-from-phase-1-inmmycar-study-of-kln-1010-in-vivo-bcma-car-t-therapy-at-the-american-society-of-hematology-ash-2025-annual-meeting/)
- **What partnerships and financings imply:** current deal flow looks more like early platform ownership and delivery-IP positioning than proof of a de-risked product class.[AbbVie/Capstan](https://news.abbvie.com/2025-06-30-AbbVie-to-Acquire-Capstan-Therapeutics%2C-Further-Strengthening-Commitment-to-Transforming-Patient-Care-in-Immunology) [Orbital Series A](https://www.orbitaltx.com/orbital-therapeutics-secures-270-million-in-series-a-financing-to-unleash-full-potential-of-rna-medicines/)
- **Next value inflection:** the field still needs human evidence of selective targeting, manageable safety, reduced conditioning burden, and durable or repeatable clinical effect.

## Strategic read-through

- **For pharma BD:** diligence should focus on delivery specificity, safety controls, and whether early human data support enough durability to justify platform ownership.
- **For platform investors:** value is likely to concentrate around delivery IP, immune-cell targeting, and evidence that in-body programming can create a durable therapeutic effect.
- **For cell therapy incumbents:** *in vivo* CAR-T is better viewed today as a strategic adjacency or hedge than as an immediate replacement for leading *ex vivo* products.

## Publicly visible strategic players

- **Kelonia:** Phase 1 anti-BCMA *in vivo* CAR-T in myeloma; acquired by Lilly.[Lilly/Kelonia](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-acquire-kelonia-therapeutics-advance-vivo-car-t-cell)
- **Capstan:** Phase 1 anti-CD19 *in vivo* CAR-T for autoimmune disease via targeted LNP / mRNA; acquired by AbbVie.[Capstan Phase 1](https://www.capstantx.com/press-releases/capstan-therapeutics-announces-initiation-of-phase-1-trial-of-lead-in-vivo-car-t-therapy-cptx2309-for-treating-autoimmune-disease/)

- **Umoja:** independent developer with lentiviral *in vivo* CAR-T programs and deals with AbbVie and Nona.[Umoja/AbbVie](https://umojabiopharma.com/umojas-in-vivo-gene-therapy-platform-selected-by-abbvie-for-an-investigational-cell-therapy-collaboration-and-option-agreement/) [Umoja/Nona](https://umojabiopharma.com/umoja-biopharma-and-nona-biosciences-expand-collaboration-to-develop-in-vivo-cell-therapies/)
- **EsoBiotec:** lentiviral ENaBL platform for in-body cell engineering; acquired by AstraZeneca.[EsoBiotec/AstraZeneca](https://www.esobiotec.com/esobiotec-to-be-acquired-by-astrazeneca-to-advance-cell-therapy-ambition/)
- **Orna / Orbital:** RNA-focused *in vivo* CAR-T platform developers, both acquired by large pharma.[Lilly/Orna](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-acquire-orna-therapeutics-advance-cell-therapies) [BMS/Orbital](https://news.bms.com/news/corporate-financial/2025/Bristol-Myers-Squibb-Strengthens-and-Diversifies-Cell-Therapy-Portfolio-with-Acquisition-of-Orbital-Therapeutics/default.aspx)

## Core risks

- Cell-specific targeting remains the central gating issue.
- Repeat dosing, immunogenicity, expression control, and long-term safety remain unresolved for much of the field.
- Many datasets remain early, company-reported, and not comparable across platforms.
- The M&A wave validates strategic interest, not broad clinical proof of concept.
